# Unpublish a Course or Report

Last updated: 2026-11-07

The local candidate adds Unpublish for saved Course and Report documents.
The [focused receipt](results/2026/11-07/workspace-unpublications-checks.md) accepts
39 core cases and four desktop/narrow browser journeys at two recorded sources.
The failed first browser verdict and its unproven abort causes remain. Hosted
activation is separate.

## Stop public access

Open the saved Course or Report. Use **Refresh publication status** to check the
current public version, then choose **Unpublish**. Review the public revision and
choose **Confirm unpublish**. Editors and administrators can unpublish; viewers
can inspect status.

After acknowledgement, new requests to the live link and all existing version
URLs are refused. The saved draft, local unsaved edits and published content are
kept. Unpublish does not require saving private edits and does not delete content.
Already-open pages, browser history snapshots, requests admitted before revocation
and copied or downloaded HTML cannot be recalled.

To publish again, apply and save the intended document, then explicitly publish
the saved revision. The live link follows the new version. Earlier revoked
version URLs remain unavailable.

## Interrupted or stale requests

If Unpublish cannot be confirmed, keep this tab open and choose **Retry same
unpublish**. Public availability remains unverified until the outcome is known.
Saving and other publication actions pause during recovery; local edits remain
visible. Retrying does not silently revoke a newer publication.

If another publication has replaced the reviewed version, the panel reports a
conflict and keeps the original acknowledgement separate from current status.
**Reload saved draft** asks before discarding local edits or unresolved work.
Historical acknowledgement URLs may already be revoked and do not establish
current public access.

This control covers canonical local Course/Report publication. Legacy Slides
publishing, deletion, hosted access/storage, public passwords/embeds/reviews,
canonical analytics and full AI/Eve remain outside this slice.
